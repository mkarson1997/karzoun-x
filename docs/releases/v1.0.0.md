# KARZOUN-X v1.0.0

Stable research-software release accompanying the completed Phase 1-13 KARZOUN-X study.

## Research evidence included

- real SMAP/MSL anomaly-detection experiments with preserved provenance and hashes
- synthetic retrieval, diagnosis, and deterministic action-gating testbeds
- local Qwen3 no-RAG versus RAG comparisons
- deterministic communication-delay and ground-link-outage analysis
- corrected single-host resource instrumentation
- precommitted Phase 9 hard-stress evaluation preserving negative results
- Phase 11 newly held-out epistemic evidence-sufficiency mitigation
- Phase 12 frozen three-model quality/resource ablation
- Phase 13 Wilson intervals, paired exact McNemar tests, and Pareto synthesis
- raw model responses, resource samples, environment metadata, machine-generated summaries, and experiment configs

## Key follow-up result

Phase 11 evaluated 108 newly held-out synthetic cases after the Phase 9 failure analysis. Baseline policy conformance was 57/108 (0.5278) and the frozen deterministic epistemic gate produced 108/108 (1.0000), with 51 gated-only correct discordant pairs and exact McNemar p = 8.881784197e-16. This result remains synthetic and does not establish flight readiness.

Phase 12 compared Qwen3 4B, 8B, and 14B on the same 36 synthetic cases per model and the unchanged Phase 11 gate. The 8B condition achieved 36/36 gated policy conformance with 9.051 s warm mean latency and 6.423 GiB peak model-process-family RSS on the measured host.

## Scope and safety boundary

KARZOUN-X is research software. It is not flight-qualified, not a certified spacecraft fault-protection system, and not authorized for real spacecraft control. Real SMAP/MSL data are used for anomaly-detection evaluation; detailed diagnosis/action experiments use a separate synthetic testbed with known labels.
