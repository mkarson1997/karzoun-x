# KARZOUN-X v0.1.0-rc1

First archival release candidate for the completed Phase 1-10 KARZOUN-X research program.

## Included research evidence

- real SMAP/MSL anomaly-detection experiments with preserved provenance and hashes
- deterministic synthetic retrieval and action-gating testbeds
- local Qwen3 14B no-RAG versus RAG comparisons
- paired Phase 7B end-to-end RAG ablation
- communication-delay and ground-link-outage analysis
- corrected single-host resource instrumentation
- precommitted Phase 9 hard-stress evaluation
- retrospective Phase 10 confidence intervals and paired McNemar synthesis
- machine-generated result artifacts, raw model responses, environment metadata, and negative/mixed findings

## Scope and safety boundary

KARZOUN-X is research software. It is not flight-qualified, not a certified spacecraft fault-protection system, and not authorized for real spacecraft control. Real SMAP/MSL data are used for anomaly-detection evaluation; later diagnosis/action experiments use a separate synthetic testbed with known labels.

The Phase 9 hard-stress result is intentionally retained: fail-safe policy conformance was `26/60 = 0.4333`, exposing weak abstention under ambiguous, conflicting, and missing evidence even though no explicitly unsafe action was proposed in that run.
